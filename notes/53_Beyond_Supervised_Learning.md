# 🚀 Beyond Supervised Learning: Advanced ML Techniques

While Supervised Learning relies on labeled data, there is a whole new frontier of powerful machine learning techniques that operate differently and solve entirely new categories of problems. 

Here are the three major advanced techniques in modern machine learning:

## 1. Unsupervised Learning
Unsupervised learning involves algorithms that draw inferences from datasets without human-provided labels (no $y$ values). These techniques are highly utilized in major commercial applications today.
*   **Clustering Algorithms:** This technique automatically groups unsorted data into distinct, meaningful clusters based on hidden patterns.
*   **Anomaly Detection:** A method used to identify unusual data points or outliers that deviate significantly from the norm, which is crucial for fraud detection or system health monitoring.

## 2. Recommender Systems
Recommender systems are among the most commercially critical machine learning technologies in existence today. Despite receiving surprisingly little attention in academic circles, they drive billions of dollars in value across the tech industry.
*   **E-commerce & Streaming:** These systems are the core engines behind online shopping websites and video streaming platforms, analyzing user behavior to recommend the right products or movies.
*   **Online Advertising:** The underlying mechanics of recommender systems are also what power massive online ad tech companies, allowing them to decide exactly which ads to show to specific users.

## 3. Reinforcement Learning
Reinforcement Learning is an emerging and highly exciting frontier in artificial intelligence. While it currently has fewer everyday commercial applications compared to the other techniques, its potential is massive.
*   **Video Games:** Reinforcement learning agents have famously mastered a variety of complex video games, often outperforming human experts.
*   **Robotics:** This technique is heavily used to control different types of physical robots in the real world.
*   **Complex Simulations:** It can be used to train software to execute highly complex maneuvers, such as autonomously landing a simulated moon lander.
